Think in outcomes

Work across boundaries

Bring curiosity to adjacent disciplines and collaborate without losing depth in your own craft. Our work rarely sits inside one specialty.

Make complexity clear

Build for real use

Working here

What the day-to-day is actually like.

Real ownership

You will own a piece of work end to end, talk to the client directly and see the result in operation.

Range of problems

A quarter might span an ERP rollout, a plant-floor control system and a data platform. Generalists and specialists both find room.

Distributed by design

We work with specialists across locations. Collaboration is written down, not dependent on being in one room.

Time to do it properly

We scope for quality including testing, documentation and handover, because we are usually the ones supporting it afterwards.

Hiring process

Four steps, and a clear answer at the end.

01

Introduction

Share your background and the problems you are strongest at solving. We read every introduction.

02

Conversation

A discussion about your discipline, how you work and what you want from the next role.

03

Practical review

A realistic exercise or portfolio walkthrough. No trick puzzles, no unpaid project work.

04

Decision

A clear answer with reasoning, whichever way it goes, and no indefinite silence.
