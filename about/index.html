A consultancy built around the whole problem.

iO Alpha brings software, data, enterprise systems, industrial automation and engineering design under one roof — because the problems our clients bring rarely respect those boundaries.

Technology and engineering, treated as one practice.

iO Alpha was formed around a simpler idea: assemble the disciplines that real operating problems actually require, and make one team accountable for the result. An ERP implementation that never connects to the plant floor has solved half a problem. A dashboard nobody trusts has solved none of it.

We work from Ohio with distributed specialists across software, quality assurance, data, ERP, industrial control, civil and automotive design, and commercial growth. We are deliberately plain about what we do and do not know — the reputation we want is the one where a client calls us back three years later.
